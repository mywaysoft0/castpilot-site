End-to-end screencast

Create episode → human review → local rendering → owner approval → private upload (YouTube Data API)

CastPilot is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel “말이 되는 영어”. This recording shows one real run, about 2.5 minutes: the owner creates an episode, reviews and edits the drafted sentences, the video is rendered on the owner’s computer, the owner previews and approves it, and the tool uploads it to the owner’s own channel as private through the YouTube Data API. Videos stay private until the owner taps “Publish”.
